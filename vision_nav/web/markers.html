Setup

Marker sheet

Six ArUco markers let the overhead camera find the arena, the robot and you. Print them once, place them, and the console takes it from there.

Print at 100% scale — turn off “fit to page”. Then check one marker with a ruler: the black square must be exactly the size written under it. Keep the white border around each marker.
  1. #0 · Robot

    On top of the robot

    Lay it flat with the arrow pointing to the robot’s front. If it ends up rotated anyway, the Calibrate button corrects it.

  2. #1 – #4 · Corners

    Around the arena

    On the floor, counter‑clockwise seen from above: #1 near‑left, #2 near‑right, #3 far‑right, #4 far‑left. Enter the centre distances #1→#2 (W) and #1→#4 (H) in the console. Best raised to the robot marker’s height.

  3. #5 · Follow me

    In your hand

    Hold it flat and facing the camera. Press Follow me in the console and the robot keeps a polite distance behind you.
