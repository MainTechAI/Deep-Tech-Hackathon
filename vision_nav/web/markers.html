Markers to print

Print at 100% scale (no "fit to page"), then check one marker with a ruler: the black square must be exactly the size written under it. Keep the white border around each marker.

  • #0 robot - flat on top of the robot, the arrow pointing to the robot's front. If it ends up rotated anyway, the Calibrate button corrects it.
  • #1-#4 corners - on the floor at the corners of the arena, counter-clockwise seen from above: #1 near-left, #2 near-right, #3 far-right, #4 far-left (as seen from the camera). Measure the distance between the centres of #1-#2 (W) and #1-#4 (H) and enter them in the console. Best: raise the corner markers to the height of the robot marker (boxes / books).
  • #5 follow me - held flat, facing the camera, by the person the robot should follow.
